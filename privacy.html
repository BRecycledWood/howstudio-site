Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 24, 2026

Overview

HOWstud.io is operated by HOW Studio LLC, a studio based in Jersey City, New Jersey. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our project inquiry process, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Short version: we collect what you send us, we use it to reply to you and do the work, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you contact us, that typically includes your name, email address, and anything else you choose to include — company name, project description, budget range, or timeline.
  • Project and client records. If we work together, we may keep correspondence, briefs, files, and project notes needed to deliver and support the work.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps) for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to build profiles of visitors.

Please don't send sensitive personal information — health details, government identifiers, financial account numbers — through the inquiry form. If a project needs that kind of data, we'll agree on a secure method first.

How we use it

  • To read and respond to your inquiry.
  • To prepare proposals, estimates, and project plans you've asked for.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve services we've been engaged to provide.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and working correctly.

We don't add you to a marketing list because you sent an inquiry, and we don't send promotional email without your permission.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate — for example web hosting, email, file storage, or payment processing — and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

If a project involves tools chosen by you (for example your CRM or a third-party platform), that provider's own privacy terms apply to data stored there.

Cookies and analytics

This website is built as a set of static pages. It does not set advertising cookies, does not run third-party tracking pixels, and does not embed social media trackers.

The site loads its fonts from this domain rather than a third-party font service, so simply visiting a page does not send your request to another company.

If we later add analytics or other measurement tools, we'll update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before those tools load.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that don't become projects are kept for as long as they're useful for follow-up, and then deleted or archived. Project records are kept for as long as needed to support the work and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

You can ask us to delete your inquiry at any time using the contact details below.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to request a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to certain uses. We'll honour those requests as required by applicable law.

To make a request, email hello@howstud.io with enough detail for us to identify your records. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards for the information we hold, including encrypted connections to this website and access controls on our own systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security — but we take the responsibility seriously.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. This website is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how we use information you've already shared will be communicated directly where we have a way to reach you.
